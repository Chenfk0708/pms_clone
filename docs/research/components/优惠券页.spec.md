## Page Topology

- PMS app shell: top navigation highlights `SCRM`.
- SCRM side navigation: visible groups are customer overview, customer management, member center, growth acquisition, marketing promotion, customer communication, enterprise WeCom staff management.
- Active side item: `营销推广 > 优惠券`.
- Business card: white AntD-style content area with two tabs, filter row, action row, empty table.
- Floating chat dock exists in the target runtime and is treated as global shell noise unless it overlaps the business area.

- Tabs: `优惠券管理` selected, `派发任务` available.
- Filter: `上架状态` select with placeholder `请选择`.

- Data state: empty, text `暂无数据`.

## Dispatch Task Tab

- Trigger: click tab `派发任务`.
- Header/action row: `全部记录`, `新建任务`.
- Table columns: `派发方式`, `优惠券`, `已派数量`, `创建时间`, `记录`.
- Data state: empty, text `暂无数据`.

## New Coupon Form

- Trigger: click `新建`.
- URL after click: `/mallManagement/couponMgt/edit`.
- Header text: `优惠券列表>新增`.
- Fields: `名称`, `类型`, `优惠金额`, `生效范围`, `领券条件`, `使用条件`, `派发上限`, `每人可领数`, `派发时间`, `时效类型`, `有效期`, `不可用时间`.
- Defaults: `类型 满减券`, `所有人可以领`, `可以与会员折扣共用`, `有效天数`.
- Footer actions: `返回列表`, `提 交`.
